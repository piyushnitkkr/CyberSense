# Cybersecurity Policy Framework

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the cybersecurity policy for organizational security posture management. The policy establishes guidelines for protecting information assets and mitigating cyber threats.

## 2. Access Control (AC)

### AC-1: Access Control Policy and Procedures
The organization shall develop, document, and disseminate an access control policy.

### AC-2: Account Management
The organization shall manage information system accounts including:
- Establishing, activating, modifying, and removing accounts
- Reviewing accounts periodically
- Coordinating with human resources for account lifecycle

### AC-3: Access Enforcement
The information system shall enforce approved authorizations for logical access to information and system resources.

## 3. Audit and Accountability (AU)

### AU-2: Event Logging
The organization shall identify events that the system is capable of auditing and coordinate the audit function.

Events to audit include:
- Successful and unsuccessful account logon events
- Access to privileged functions
- Changes to security policy
- System administrator actions

### AU-6: Audit Review, Analysis, and Reporting
The organization shall review and analyze system audit records for indications of inappropriate activity.

## 4. System and Communications Protection (SC)

### SC-8: Transmission Confidentiality and Integrity
The information system shall protect the confidentiality and integrity of transmitted information.

### SC-13: Cryptographic Protection
The information system shall implement cryptographic mechanisms to protect information at rest and in transit.

## 5. Incident Response (IR)

### IR-1: Incident Response Policy and Procedures
The organization shall develop and disseminate an incident response policy and procedures.

### IR-4: Incident Handling
The organization shall implement incident handling capability including:
- Preparation
- Detection and analysis
- Containment, eradication, and recovery
- Post-incident activity

## 6. Risk Assessment (RA)

### RA-3: Risk Assessment
The organization shall conduct risk assessments to identify threats and vulnerabilities.

### RA-5: Vulnerability Scanning
The organization shall scan for vulnerabilities in the information system periodically.

## 7. Security Awareness and Training (AT)

### AT-2: Security Awareness
The organization shall train all users on security awareness including:
- Recognizing phishing attempts
- Reporting security incidents
- Proper password management
- Physical security awareness

## 8. Configuration Management (CM)

### CM-3: Configuration Change Control
The organization shall control changes to the information system configuration.

### CM-6: Configuration Settings
The organization shall establish and maintain configuration settings for system components.

## 9. Contingency Planning (CP)

### CP-2: Contingency Plan
The organization shall develop a contingency plan that identifies essential missions and business functions.

### CP-9: Information System Backup
The organization shall conduct backups of user-level and system-level information.

## 10. Identification and Authentication (IA)

### IA-2: Identification and Authentication
The organization shall identify and authenticate user identities before allowing access to the system.

### IA-5: Authenticator Management
The organization shall manage authenticators including:
- Verifying identity before issuing authenticators
- Establishing authenticator parameters
- Changing authenticators periodically

## Conclusion

This cybersecurity policy framework provides the foundation for protecting organizational assets. Regular review and updates are essential to address evolving threats.
